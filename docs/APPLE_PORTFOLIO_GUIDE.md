(ALTERNATIVE) PROJECT: MENTOR BELAJARKU
Title: Mentor Belajarku - Integrated Tutoring Management & Smart Attendance System
Year: 2026
Role: Lead System Architect & Full-Stack Developer
Link: https://github.com/fikri210604/mentorbelajarku
Asset Foto: src/assets/projects/mentorbelajarku.png

Description:
Tutoring centers frequently face operational confusion caused by unverified paper attendance, routine schedule clashes, and error-prone manual tutor payroll calculations in spreadsheets. To resolve this, I researched administrative bottlenecks across management, tutors, and parents, modeling a decoupled architecture separating routine schedules from actual calendar sessions and identifying the need for historical rate locking. I engineered a production-ready web platform using Next.js 15, TypeScript, and Supabase featuring in-browser camera-based photo attendance with client-side image compression, role-based access control via Better Auth, and an automated payroll calculation engine. The system eliminates human calculation errors through historical effective dating, provides parents with tamper-proof proof of learning, and ensures end-to-end operational transparency with detailed audit logging.
